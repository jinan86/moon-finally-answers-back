Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) competition **The Moon Finally Answers Back** — one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> You texted your ex at midnight and I'm the one they call unstable.

The Moon has watched every 3 a.m. bad decision for as long as anyone can remember — and somehow *it's* the one people call unstable. Luna, lunatic: the blame gets handed straight back.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Output: 66 characters, one line, UTF-8.
